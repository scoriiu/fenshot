What you get from one paste

fenshot reads the screenshot and hands you three things: the FEN string ready to copy, a live board you can play on with a free Stockfish engine, and one-click export to Lichess if you prefer analyzing there.

Works with the screenshots you actually have

chess.com and Lichess in any board theme, mobile app captures, stream and video frames, and printed diagrams photographed from a book. The recognizer was trained on the piece sets chess sites and publishers really use.

What is FEN, in one paragraph

FEN (Forsyth–Edwards Notation) is the universal one-line text format for a chess position: piece placement, side to move, castling rights. Every serious chess tool accepts it. Once you have the FEN, the position travels anywhere: engines, databases, coaching sessions, forum posts.

Fixing an imperfect scan

Hard-to-read piece sets sometimes produce a wrong square or two. The built-in editor shows the original image next to the board so you can correct squares in seconds, then copy the corrected FEN.
