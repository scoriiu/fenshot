The one-paste flow

  1. Screenshot the chess.com board: browser, app, or a frame from a streamer's video.
  2. Paste it on fenshot.com.
  3. Click the Lichess button for a Lichess analysis board, or analyze right here with a local Stockfish, or copy the FEN for anywhere else.

Any theme, both sides

The reader handles chess.com's board themes and piece sets, and detects which side is at the bottom, so positions from either player's perspective come out correct.

For repeat use: the extension

If you do this daily, the fenshot browser extension skips the screenshot: one click on any page with a visible board reads it directly. Works on chess.com, Lichess, YouTube and news articles.

fenshot is MIT open source. The analysis board is powered by coachess.app, where a coach can also review your full games.
