Chess board scanner

A chess board scanner turns a picture of a position into the position itself: every piece on its square, on a board you can analyze. fenshot does it in your browser, free, in about a second.

Try it now, paste your image →

What can it scan?

  • Screenshots from chess.com and Lichess, in any theme.
  • Frames from YouTube videos and streams.
  • Diagrams from chess books and PDFs.
  • Puzzles posted on Reddit or sent in a chat.
  • A photo of a real board taken from straight above can work. Photos taken at an angle are unreliable.

What you get

  • The position on a board, so you can check it against the picture.
  • The FEN, the one line of text every chess site and engine reads as a position, ready to copy.
  • One click to analyze it with a free engine on Kibitzo or on Lichess.

How to scan a chess board

  1. Copy the picture or take a screenshot.
  2. Paste it on fenshot.com (Ctrl+V works anywhere on the page) or drop the file in.
  3. Check the board, set the side to move, and analyze or copy the position.

When the scan is not sure

A scanner that is silently wrong is worse than no scanner. fenshot tells you when a read is doubtful, and when a position cannot be legal it sends you to the Lichess editor to fix it. How often it is right, measured on real images with the weak spots named, is on the accuracy page.

Scan the page you are on

If you scan positions often, the fenshot browser extension reads the board straight off the page in one click, with no screenshot: videos, articles, studies, and whole games where the page shows the moves.

It stays on your machine

The recognition model runs in your browser via WebAssembly. The image is never uploaded, which is why it works offline once loaded and why there is no account, no quota and no cost.

More ways to use fenshot

  • Best move from a chess screenshot: scan the position, then ask the engine
  • Chess screenshot to FEN: chess.com, Lichess or video screenshots
  • Chess image to board: any picture of a position
  • Photo of a chess board to FEN: a real board or a printed page
  • Chess book diagram to FEN: diagrams from books and PDFs
  • Chess.com screenshot to Lichess: move a position between sites
  • The fenshot browser extension: read the board straight off any page, and whole games
fenshot is MIT open source. The analysis board is powered by kibitzo.com, where a coach can also review your full games.
